Disclaimer

Experimental research product, not for operational dispatch decisions.

These forecasts are produced by a research system that is still being developed and evaluated. The daily (interim) mode has not been tested in live use. Forecasts can be wrong, late, incomplete or out of date, and some areas are flagged as unreliable.

The forecasts are provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, express or implied, including fitness for a particular purpose. Western Kentucky University, the AIR Lab and the authors accept no liability for any decision made or action taken using them. For grid operations, use the forecasts of the responsible balancing authority or system operator.

Replays (hindcasts) re-run the system on past dates and are clearly marked. They are not forecasts that were issued at the time.
