All the numbers

How to read this

  • The median forecast line: half of outcomes fall above it, half below.
  • The 50% range (darker band) holds the middle half of outcomes; the 90% range (lighter band) holds nine in ten.
  • The dashed line is this area's top-10% demand level for the time of year. Switch it to the top 5% or top 1% above the chart.
  • The observed line is what demand actually was. This is a hindcast, so you can check the forecast against it.
