Stable paths for scripts

These always point at the newest run, live or not. Check kind in the summary: live for a real forecast, replay for a hindcast.

  • data/issues.json: list of available runs, newest first
  • data/latest/summary.json: run details and one row per area
  • data/latest/risk.json: chances by area and day, for all three levels
  • data/<issue date>/<AREA>.json and .csv: one area, every percentile
  • data/<issue date>/forecast_all.csv: every area in one file

What the columns mean

The CSV files have one row per area, quantity and day (or week).

CSV column dictionary
ColumnMeaning
issueDate the forecast was issued (YYYY-MM-DD).
baBalancing authority code, for example TVA or ERCO.
targetpeak (highest hourly demand of the day or week) or energy (total demand of the day or week).
unitLead code, not a physical unit: d1–d14 are days after the issue date, w1–w6 are weeks (w1 = d1–d7). Live runs have w1–w2 only.
valid_start, valid_endFirst and last date the row covers (same date for days). Per-area files only.
value_unitPhysical unit: MW for peak; MWh for energy (per day for d leads, per week for w leads). Per-area files only.
observedActual demand, in replays only. Empty in live runs.
lag_obs_missingTrue if recent observed demand was missing when the forecast was made.
q05 … q95Forecast percentiles in steps of 5: 5% of outcomes are expected below q05, half below q50, 95% below q95.
t90, t95, t99The area's top-10%, top-5% and top-1% demand levels for that time of year. Peak only.
p_gt_t90, p_gt_t95, p_gt_t99Forecast chance (0 to 1) that demand exceeds t90, t95 or t99. Peak only.
labelRun description (live runs in forecast_all.csv only).
